MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of 1 March 2025 (the "Effective Date") by and between:

Northwind Analytics Ltd., a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 08812345, whose registered office is at 14 Harbour Street, Bristol BS1 4QA (the "Supplier"); and

Bluefield Retail plc, a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 01234567, whose registered office is at 200 Kingsway, London WC2B 6NH (the "Customer").

The parties agree as follows:

1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 Definitions. In this Agreement the following terms have the following meanings: "Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in England; "Charges" means the fees payable by the Customer for the Services as set out in Schedule 2; "Deliverables" means all documents, reports and materials developed by the Supplier as part of the Services; "Services" means the data analytics services described in Schedule 1; "Statement of Work" means a document agreed in writing by both parties describing specific Services to be provided.

1.2 Interpretation. Clause headings shall not affect the interpretation of this Agreement. A reference to writing or written includes email. Words in the singular include the plural and vice versa.

2. TERM

2.1 Initial Term. This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue for an initial term of twenty-four (24) months (the "Initial Term").

2.2 Renewal. On expiry of the Initial Term this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive periods of twelve (12) months (each a "Renewal Term") unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the Initial Term or the then-current Renewal Term.

3. SERVICES

3.1 Provision of Services. The Supplier shall provide the Services to the Customer in accordance with this Agreement and each Statement of Work, using reasonable skill and care and in accordance with good industry practice.

3.2 Service Levels. The Supplier shall meet the service levels set out in Schedule 3. The Supplier shall provide the Customer with a monthly report on its performance against the service levels within
5 days of the end of each calendar month.

3.3 Supplier Obligations. The Supplier shall:
1. co-operate with the Customer in all matters relating to the Services;
2. appoint a manager for the Services who shall have authority to bind the Supplier on day-to-day matters;
3. comply with all applicable laws and the Customer's reasonable security policies notified in writing.

4. CHARGES AND PAYMENT

4.1 Charges. In consideration of the Services, the Customer shall pay the Charges set out in Schedule 2. The Charges are exclusive of VAT, which shall be payable by the Customer in addition at the applicable rate.

4.2 Invoicing. The Supplier shall invoice the Customer monthly in arrears. The Customer shall pay each valid invoice within thirty (30) days of the date of receipt of the invoice.

4.3 Late Payment. If the Customer fails to pay any undisputed amount by the due date, the Supplier may charge interest on the overdue amount at the rate of 4% per annum above the Bank of England base rate from the due date until the date of actual payment.

5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

5.1 Ownership. All intellectual property rights in the Deliverables shall vest in the Customer upon payment in full of the Charges relating to those Deliverables.

5.2 Supplier Materials. The Supplier retains ownership of its pre-existing materials, tools and know-how and grants the Customer a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to use them to the extent necessary to receive the benefit of the Services.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY

6.1 Each party shall keep confidential all information disclosed to it by the other party concerning the business, customers or suppliers of the other party, and shall not use such information except to perform its obligations under this Agreement. This obligation shall survive termination of this Agreement for a period of three (3) years.

7. DATA PROTECTION

7.1 The Supplier shall process personal data only on the documented instructions of the Customer and in accordance with the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

7.2 The Supplier shall notify the Customer without undue delay, and in any event within forty-eight (48) hours, after becoming aware of a personal data breach.

8. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

8.1 Nothing in this Agreement limits or excludes liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, for fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation, or for any other liability which cannot be limited or excluded by law.

8.2 Subject to clause 8.1, neither party shall be liable for any loss of profits, loss of business, or any indirect or consequential loss arising under or in connection with this Agreement.

8.3 Subject to clause 8.1, each party's total aggregate liability arising under or in connection with this Agreement shall be limited to 125% of the total Charges paid or payable in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

9. TERMINATION

9.1 Termination for Convenience. The Customer may terminate this Agreement or any Statement of Work for convenience by giving the Supplier not less than sixty (60) days' written notice.

9.2 Termination for Cause. Either party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by giving written notice to the other party if the other party commits a material breach of this Agreement which is irremediable or, if remediable, fails to remedy that breach within thirty (30) days of being notified in writing to do so.

9.3 Consequences of Termination. On termination the Customer shall pay all outstanding invoices and, for Services provided but not yet invoiced, the Supplier may submit an invoice which shall be payable within thirty (30) days.

10. NOTICES

10.1 Any notice given under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, by pre-paid first-class post or by email to the address of the relevant party set out at the head of this Agreement.

10.2 A notice delivered by email shall be deemed received at 9.00 am on the next Business Day after transmission.

11. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

11.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the law of England and Wales.

11.2 Each party irrevocably agrees that the courts of England and Wales shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
